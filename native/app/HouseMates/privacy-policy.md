# Privacy policy for HouseMates

Last updated 13 October 2026. This is a translation of the Swedish privacy policy (integritetspolicy.md), which applies if the two differ.

HouseMates is an app for two people who share a household: a shopping list, to-dos, chores, dinners and a shared calendar. The data controller is [NAME]. Send questions about your data to [EMAIL].

## What we store

- **Account:** email address, name and password. The password is stored encrypted and cannot be read by anyone.
- **Household:** which household you belong to, the household's invite code and your colour in the app.
- **What you add:** the shopping list, tasks, chores and dinners with ingredients, as well as who did what and when.
- **Notifications:** an address for your phone for push notifications, your quiet hours and your time zone.

## The calendar

The calendar's events are in your own calendar on your phone (for example iCloud) and are not sent to us. We only store:

- the name of the calendar you share and the ids of a few events in it, so that your partner's phone finds the same calendar
- who an event is for, if you mark it
- the title and start time of events you create, change or delete in the app, so that your partner can get a notification

The app only reads and writes in the calendar you have chosen to share.

## Recipes from a link

When you import a recipe, our server fetches the page behind the link. We store the recipe's name, ingredients, image link and the link. A link you share from Safari stays on your phone until the app has fetched the recipe.

## Why we store the data

The data is only used to make the app work: so that you and your partner see the same things, and to send notifications. We show no ads, do not track you and do not sell or hand over data to anyone else.

## Where the data is

The data is stored with Supabase on servers within the EU. Emails from the app, such as the link to confirm your account, are sent via Resend, and push notifications via Apple. They process the data on our behalf and may not use it for anything else.

## How long we keep the data

- The history of what has happened is kept for 90 days.
- Deleted items, tasks, chores and dinners are removed completely after 30 days.
- Tasks that have been completed for 30 days are deleted.
- Everything else is kept until you delete your account.

## Delete your data

Under Settings → Delete account, your account, your name, your history and your phone's notification address are deleted immediately.

- If your partner stays in the household, what you added together stays for your partner, without any link to you.
- If you are alone in the household, the whole household is deleted.
- The events in your calendar are not affected, since we don't have them.

## Your rights

You have the right to know what data we have about you, to have errors corrected, to have the data deleted and to get it in a format you can use elsewhere. Contact us at [EMAIL]. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
